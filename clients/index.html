Lightlag

A co-op Mars mission game. Design the mission. Then live with it at the speed of light.

Up to four players. Two in Houston, two aboard a ship to Mars with a crew of four. You design the mission together and a review board checks it. Then you fly it apart: every word between Earth and the ship crosses space at the speed of light, on the real dates of the 2020 launch window, and arrives minutes old. In flight, the game tells you which of your choices decided what. Any seat nobody takes is played by AI. Playing alone? Take Flight Director.

The demo plays itself in its own room: design, the review board, the storm, the silence behind the Sun, the debrief. Take any seat whenever you like. A new mission opens the Flight Director seat; invite the others from there.
